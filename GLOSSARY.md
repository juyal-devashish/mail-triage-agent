# Glossary

**Conversation**: a set of emails that reply to each other (a Gmail thread). The conversation is what gets triaged, archived and moved; a single email is never judged on its own.

**Verdict**: Claude's call on one conversation, judged on its latest email with the earlier ones as context: which section it belongs in, its category, a one-line summary, why, the next step and any due date.

**Needs you**: the section for conversations where someone is waiting on you, or something has a deadline or a real consequence if ignored. If you sent the last reply, the ball is in the other person's court and the conversation doesn't need you, unless a due date is coming up.

**Worth knowing**: the section for conversations worth a glance that ask nothing of you.

**Noise**: the section for conversations you can skip: newsletters, promotions, routine notifications, receipts and phishing.

**Digest**: noise of one category, folded into a single row you can archive in one go.

**Category**: the kind of conversation (reply, deadline, meeting, bill, job, newsletter, promo and so on). It groups noise into digests; it never decides the section by itself.

**Job**: the category for your job search: recruiters, applications, assessments, interviews and offers.

**Job leads**: the digest for job-board alerts and mass recruiter postings: noise, but kept findable.

**Triage label**: the single Gmail label the app keeps in step with Needs you ("Triage/Needs you"), so the short list is visible in Gmail on your phone.

**Sender rule**: a standing override you create by moving a conversation: mail from that address (or domain) always goes to the section you chose.

**Profile**: your standing context, written by you and kept on your machine: the people who always matter, what you're working on, and what is always noise for you. Claude reads it for every verdict.

**Retention**: how long the app keeps a conversation's text on your machine: while it's in your inbox, plus 30 days. After that only its ID, its verdict and what your sender rules need are kept.

**Spend cap**: the most the app may spend on Claude in a calendar month. Once it's reached, nothing more is triaged until the next month.
